RIVERA v. COASTLINE MOVING COMPANY (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)
DEPOSITION OF MARIA LOPEZ
All people, companies and events in this file are invented for demonstration.
[Page 1]
 1  Q. Please state your name for the record.
 2  A. Maria Lopez.
 3  Q. Where are you employed?
 4  A. I work as a warehouse supervisor at Coastline
 5  Moving Company in Long Beach, California.
 6  Q. How long have you held that position?
 7  A. About six years. I started as a loader in March
 8  2016 and was promoted to supervisor in 2018.
 9  Q. Were you working on the afternoon of October 14,
10  2024?
11  A. Yes. I was on the day shift, from 7:00 a.m. to 3:30
12  p.m.
13  MR. DIAZ: Objection, form. You may answer.
14  Q. Where were you at about 2:15 p.m. that day?
15  A. I was near Loading Dock 4, checking the manifest
16  for a delivery truck.
17  Q. Did you see Mr. Rivera that afternoon?
18  A. Yes. He was carrying a box down the main aisle
19  toward Dock 4.
20  Q. Describe what you saw happen.
21  A. He stepped around a pallet jack and slipped on what
22  looked like a wet patch on the concrete floor. He fell
23  on his right side.
24  Q. How large was the wet patch?
25  A. Maybe three feet across. It looked like water or
[Page 2]
 1  maybe a cleaning solution.
 2  Q. Was there a wet floor sign near the patch?
 3  A. No. I did not see any sign in that part of the
 4  aisle.
 5  Q. Who was responsible for placing signs?
 6  A. The cleaning crew placed signs when they mopped.
 7  Supervisors were supposed to check the aisles each
 8  shift too.
 9  Q. Did you check the aisle near Dock 4 that day before
10  the incident?
11  A. I walked through at about 1:00 p.m. and did not
12  notice anything.
13  Q. Did you see the pallet jack in the aisle before the
14  incident?
15  A. No, I did not. It was not there when I walked
16  through at 1:00.
17  Q. What happened right after Mr. Rivera fell?
18  A. I called for the first aid attendant on the radio
19  and stayed with him until she arrived, about four
20  minutes later.
21  Q. Did Mr. Rivera say anything to you?
22  A. He said his hip hurt badly and that he could not
23  stand up.
24  Q. Was an incident report filled out?
25  A. Yes. I filled out the report myself that evening
[Page 3]
 1  and gave it to the safety manager, Tom Becker.
 2  Q. Did you take photographs?
 3  A. I took three photographs with my phone before the
 4  crew cleaned the area.
 5  Q. Have you spoken to anyone at the company about this
 6  incident?
 7  A. I spoke with Mr. Becker once, and with the
 8  company's lawyer last week to prepare for today.
 9  Q. Has the company changed any procedures since?
10  A. Yes. Starting in November 2024, supervisors must
11  sign an aisle inspection log every two hours.
12  Q. So you saw the pallet jack in the aisle before the
13  incident?
14  A. Yes, I saw it as I turned the corner, parked near
15  Dock 4.
16  Q. Which is it?
17  A. I may be mixing up the order. I remember the pallet
18  jack but I cannot say exactly when I first noticed it.
19  Q. Do you know whether the cleaning crew mopped that
20  aisle that day?
21  A. I believe they mopped around noon, but I do not
22  have a record of it.
23  Q. Do you know who the crew member was?
24  A. I think it was Luis Ortega, but I am not certain.
25  Q. Is there anything you would like to add?
